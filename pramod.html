Pricing, Monetization & Growth IndustryBuying.com | 2019–2024

One price doesn't fit every buyer

Baseline 18% repeat visit rate, flat non-personalized pricing
Segment Price-sensitive buyers, enterprise bulk accounts
Timeframe 6 months post-launch
Problem

Every buyer saw the same price. Price-sensitive buyers left. Large accounts had no reason to buy in bulk.

What I built
  • MyPrice, an AI pricing tool that adjusts to each buyer's price sensitivity.
  • Tiered bulk pricing for enterprise accounts.
  • An NPS program with a closed feedback loop.
Repeat visits 18% → 23% +20% average order value +15% NPS +12% conversion
Supply Chain, 3PL & Network IndustryBuying.com | 2019–2024

Cheaper shipping, fewer stockouts, better margins

Baseline Pre-automation fulfillment and manual replenishment
Segment Fragile SKUs, 9% of GMV; 30% of shipment volume
Timeframe 8–12 months per initiative
Problem

Fragile products failed delivery. Shipping cost too much per order. Pick locations ran out of stock. Prices ignored what inventory actually cost.

What I built
  • Pincode-based hyperlocal order routing for fragile SKUs.
  • Grouped-shipment logic to combine orders.
  • Demand-led auto replenishment for pick locations.
  • Lot-level cost pricing on high-margin SKUs.
+20% sales across 9% of GMV −15% shipping cost per order −40% purchase order effort +12% margin
Procurement, Planning & Finance IndustryBuying.com | 2019–2024

Automating how we buy and get paid

Baseline Manual vendor planning, 15-day sourcing cycle
Segment 1.2M SKUs; 40% of GMV on COD-risk orders
Timeframe 6–12 months
Problem

Vendor planning was manual across 1.2 million SKUs. Cash-on-delivery orders caused returns and payment risk. Sourcing took weeks.

What I built
  • ProcMan, an automated vendor planning and procurement tool.
  • PaySecure with PartPay to cut COD risk.
  • A reverse-auction system for strategic spend.
  • Invoice-level payment adjustments.
−10% procurement cost +20% payment realization Sourcing 15 → 8 days −50% manual tracking
